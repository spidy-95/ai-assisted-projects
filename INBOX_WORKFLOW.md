# AI-Powered Email Triage and Work Prioritization Assistant

Status: initial workflow demonstrated; recurring monitoring not configured.

1. Review recent email threads through a connected inbox.
2. Read conversation history before proposing action.
3. Separate emails needing replies from announcements, optional acknowledgments, and tasks requiring action outside email.
4. Skip messages already answered.
5. Prepare concise, context-aware replies without inventing commitments or facts.
6. Keep drafts for human review; do not send automatically.

Observed examples included avoiding a duplicate reply to an already answered conversation and recognizing that a training notice required training rather than an email response. Drafts were presented for review; no automated sending or Gmail draft creation is claimed.

This file documents the workflow, not a standalone application or deployable inbox agent.
